---
title: "Install the extension"
description: "Get Matchr onto LinkedIn job postings in under a minute"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add it from the Chrome Web Store">
    Open the [Matchr listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/anohdbkgehgbnbggnajlpefjapebbkok) and click **Add to Chrome**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin it to your toolbar">
    Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, find **Matchr**, and click the pin icon so it's always one click away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a LinkedIn job posting">
    Go to any job posting on `linkedin.com/jobs/...` and click the Matchr icon. The side panel opens on the right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Matchr only activates on LinkedIn job posting pages. It doesn't read anything else you browse.
</Note>

The first time you open it, the panel asks you to connect your Matchr account. That's the next step.
