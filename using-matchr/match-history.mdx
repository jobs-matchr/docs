title: "Match history and the dashboard"
description: "Where your past matches go, and how to browse all of them"
---

Every job Matchr scores gets saved to your match history automatically.

## In the side panel

The panel shows your 5 most recent matches, each with the job title, company, and score. Click any of them to instantly revisit that result without re-scoring, no credit used.

![History list at the bottom of the side panel, with two past matches and their scores](/images/side-panel-result-bottom.png)

## In the dashboard

For your full history, click **See all match history** at the bottom of the panel's list, or go to **Match history** in the [dashboard](https://matchr.in/dashboard/history) directly. There you can:

- Search by job title or company
- Filter to only the matches worth applying to
- See every match you've ever run, not just the 5 most recent

![Match history page in the Matchr dashboard](/images/match-history.png)

The panel and dashboard read from the same history, so nothing is duplicated or out of sync between them.
