Matchr runs on a credit pool rather than a subscription. Every plan is a one-time purchase, there's no auto-renewal and nothing to cancel.

| Plan | Price | Credits | Validity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free Trial | Free | 15 credits, granted once at signup | Never expires |
| 7-Day Sprint | ₹99 | 35 credits | 7 days from purchase |
| 30-Day Pass | ₹349 | 150 credits | 30 days from purchase |

All three plans work identically otherwise, same extension, same dashboard, same resume optimizer. The difference is how many credits you have and, for the paid passes, how long you have to use them.

<Warning>
  Sprint and 30-Day Pass credits expire with the pass. Any credits left unused when the validity window ends are forfeited and your account reverts to the Free Trial tier. Free Trial credits don't expire on their own.
</Warning>

See [How credits work](/plans/how-credits-work) for what actually spends a credit.

## Buying or topping up

Manage your plan from **Payments** in the [dashboard](https://matchr.in/dashboard/payments), where you can see your current balance, purchase a pass, and view past invoices.
