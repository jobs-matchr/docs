---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Common questions about Matchr"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What file types can I upload?">
    PDF and DOCX, up to 10MB. If your resume has a complex or multi-column layout, Matchr reads it visually rather than as a raw text dump, so the extracted text keeps the right reading order.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Matchr work on job boards other than LinkedIn?">
    Not currently. The extension only activates on LinkedIn job posting pages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I keep more than one resume in Matchr?">
    Yes. Upload as many as you like and switch which one is active from the dropdown in the side panel, see [Switching between resumes](/using-matchr/switching-resumes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does revisiting a job I've already scored cost another credit?">
    No. Matchr caches the result for that resume and that job. You're only charged the first time a specific resume is scored against a specific posting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the resume optimizer guarantee a higher score?">
    No, and it's built deliberately not to fake one. It re-runs the same real scoring pipeline used everywhere in Matchr against your edited resume and shows you whatever it actually computes. Most genuine, defensible improvements do raise the score, but it's a real result, not a promise.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the optimizer invent things about my experience?">
    No. It only reworks wording and emphasis around what's already in your resume. If a stronger edit would need a fact it can't verify, like a specific metric or who owned a budget, it asks you instead of guessing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I run out of credits?">
    You'll see a reminder in the side panel once your balance is low. Once it hits zero, scoring a new job or finalizing an optimized resume will ask you to top up first, nothing you've already done is affected. See [How credits work](/plans/how-credits-work).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password. How do I reset it?">
    Automatic password reset isn't set up yet. Email [jobs.matchr@gmail.com](mailto:jobs.matchr@gmail.com) and we'll help you regain access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    Email [jobs.matchr@gmail.com](mailto:jobs.matchr@gmail.com) and we'll take care of it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
