<Update label="v1.0.2">
  **Resume Optimizer.** A new chat-based editor that tailors your resume to one specific job, then re-scores it for real. See [What the resume optimizer does](/optimizer/overview).

  **Resume switcher.** Keep more than one resume on file and choose which is active from the side panel, no re-uploading to switch back and forth.

  **Fixed a side panel reliability issue.** A job detected right as the panel was still starting up could get silently dropped, occasionally needing a reload or reopen to show up. It's now buffered and applied the moment the panel finishes loading.

  **Days-remaining badge.** Sprint and 30-Day Pass users now see how many days are left before their credits expire.

  **Free Trial credits raised** from 5 to 15, enough to try several matches and still test the resume optimizer.

  **Cleaner resume exports.** Optimized resumes now download as a properly formatted, ATS-friendly PDF with real visual hierarchy, and resumes with complex layouts are now read visually on upload instead of as raw extracted text, fixing garbled or scrambled text for multi-column designs.
</Update>

<Update label="v1.0.1">
  Added a loading animation for resume upload and updates, fixed a dashboard checklist bug, and general reliability improvements.
</Update>

<Update label="v1.0.0">
  Initial public release: real-time match scoring in the LinkedIn side panel, account connection via API key, and the Matchr credit system.
</Update>
